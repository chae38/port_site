01 / About

Across disciplines.
Focused on health.

My research perspective is shaped by an unusual intersection of nursing, international relations, and climate and planetary health. I began in clinical care, moved into evidence-based environmental health assessment, and developed a research focus on the health consequences of environmental change.

I am particularly interested in connecting epidemiological evidence with the realities of environmental governance — and in using spatial data to understand where climate-related health risks emerge and who may be most affected.

02 / Featured Research

Research at the climate–health interface.

MSc Research Project

Dryland Restoration and Onchocerciasis in Africa

Investigating the spatial relationship between Great Green Wall restoration sites and the landscape of onchocerciasis endemicity and prevalence, drawing on geospatial datasets and environmental indicators.

Methods and tools: R, QGIS, spatial data integration, environmental covariates and exploratory geographic analysis.

Observational spatial research; no causal effect of restoration is claimed.
